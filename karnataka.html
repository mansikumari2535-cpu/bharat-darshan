WELCOME TO KARNATAKA

The Land of Heritage and Natural Wonders

Karnataka is a state in southern India with a rich history, diverse geography and distinctive cultural traditions. From the historic ruins of Hampi to the modern energy of Bengaluru, the state brings together heritage and contemporary life.

Karnataka is known for its contributions to Indian architecture, literature, music, classical dance, handicrafts and cuisine. Its landscape includes the Western Ghats, fertile plains, forests and a long Arabian Sea coastline.

Famous Places to Visit

Hampi

A UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring the remarkable ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, including temples, stone structures and historic market streets.

Mysuru Palace

One of India's most celebrated royal palaces, known for its grand architecture, detailed interiors and spectacular illumination during Dasara.

Coorg (Kodagu)

A scenic hill region famous for coffee plantations, green landscapes, waterfalls and distinctive Kodava culture.

Jog Falls

One of India's best-known waterfalls, formed by the Sharavathi River as it descends through a dramatic rocky landscape.

Badami Cave Temples

Historic rock-cut temples featuring intricate sculptures and architecture from the Chalukya period.

Belur and Halebidu

Famous for their exquisite Hoysala temples, detailed stone carvings and remarkable medieval craftsmanship.

Gokarna

A coastal destination known for its beaches, temples and scenic surroundings along the Arabian Sea.

Bandipur National Park

An important wildlife reserve in the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve, known for elephants, tigers and diverse forest life.

Pattadakal

A UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring a group of historic Hindu and Jain temples that showcase early Indian temple architecture.

Chikmagalur

A beautiful hill destination known for coffee estates, mountain scenery and trekking landscapes.

Traditional Karnataka Food

Bisi Bele Bath

A flavourful one-pot dish made with rice, lentils, vegetables and a distinctive spice blend.

Mysore Masala Dosa

A popular crispy dosa with a spicy chutney layer, usually served with sambar and coconut chutney.

Ragi Mudde

A traditional finger millet preparation commonly served with sambar or regional vegetable curries.

Neer Dosa

A soft, thin rice crepe associated with coastal Karnataka and often served with chutney or curry.

Akki Rotti

A rice-flour flatbread prepared with spices and sometimes vegetables, popular in Karnataka homes.

Mysore Pak

A famous sweet from Mysuru made using gram flour, ghee and sugar, known for its rich texture.

Udupi Sambar

A well-known regional preparation with lentils, vegetables and a distinctive roasted spice mixture.

Holige (Obbattu)

A festive sweet flatbread filled with a mixture such as lentils and jaggery.

Festivals and Celebrations

Mysuru Dasara

A grand ten-day celebration featuring royal traditions, cultural programmes, illuminated landmarks and processions.

Ugadi

The Kannada and Telugu New Year celebration, observed with traditional food, family gatherings and festive rituals.

Karaga Festival

A historic Bengaluru celebration associated with the worship of Draupadi and traditional night processions.

Hampi Utsav

A cultural festival celebrating the heritage of Hampi through music, dance, art and performances.

Kambala

A traditional buffalo race festival held in parts of coastal Karnataka, deeply connected with local agricultural culture.

Art, Music and Cultural Heritage

Karnataka has a rich artistic heritage. Mysore paintings, Channapatna wooden toys, sandalwood carving, Bidriware and Ilkal textiles are among its distinctive crafts.

Yakshagana is a celebrated traditional theatre form combining music, dance, elaborate costumes and storytelling. Carnatic music and classical dance traditions also have a strong presence in the state.

Best Time to Explore Karnataka

October to March is generally comfortable for exploring many parts of Karnataka. The hill regions and Western Ghats are especially green during the monsoon, although heavy rainfall can affect travel conditions.
