WELCOME TO THE TERRITORY

About Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu

Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is a Union Territory on the western coast of India. It was formed on 26 January 2020 by merging the former Union Territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

The territory has three geographically separate parts: Dadra and Nagar Haveli, Daman, and Diu. Its administrative headquarters is located at Daman.

HISTORY

A Rich and Diverse Past

Daman and Diu remained under Portuguese rule for centuries before becoming part of India in December 1961. Dadra and Nagar Haveli were liberated from Portuguese control in 1954 and later became a Union Territory in 1961.

On 26 January 2020, the two former Union Territories were merged to create the present Union Territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

Famous Tourist Destinations

Diu Fort

A historic coastal fort built during Portuguese rule, offering views of the Arabian Sea.

Naida Caves

An interesting network of caves near Diu Fort, known for their natural formations and passages.

Devka Beach

A popular coastal destination in Daman, known for its seaside atmosphere and promenade.

Jampore Beach

A scenic beach in Daman with a peaceful coastal setting.

Silvassa

The principal town of Dadra and Nagar Haveli, known for green surroundings and cultural attractions.

Vanganga Lake Garden

A landscaped garden near Silvassa with a lake, bridges and pleasant walking areas.

CULTURE & TRADITIONS

A Blend of Communities

The territory has a diverse cultural identity shaped by tribal communities, Gujarati traditions and Portuguese historical influences. Warli art is an important traditional art form associated with communities in the region.

Gujarati, Hindi, English and several local languages are used across different communities.

FOOD & FLAVOURS

Local Cuisine

The local food culture reflects Gujarati, coastal and Portuguese influences. Seafood, traditional Gujarati dishes, rice preparations and coconut-based flavours are found in the region.

NATURE & ENVIRONMENT

Coasts, Forests and Wildlife

The territory includes coastal stretches along the Arabian Sea as well as forested landscapes around Dadra and Nagar Haveli. These varied environments support different plants, birds and other wildlife.
