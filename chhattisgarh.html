Chhattisgarh

The Heart of India, known for its forests, waterfalls, tribal traditions and vibrant folk culture.

Discover Chhattisgarh

A Land of Forests and Living Traditions

Chhattisgarh is a central Indian state known for its mineral resources, dense forests, rivers, waterfalls and rich indigenous heritage. Its cultural identity is shaped by many communities, traditional crafts, folk music and distinctive festivals.

Explore the state

Famous Places

Discover ancient temples, beautiful waterfalls, caves and remarkable natural landscapes.

Chitrakote Falls

A spectacular waterfall on the Indravati River, often called the Niagara of India.

Tirathgarh Falls

A scenic waterfall in Kanger Valley National Park, surrounded by forest and rocky landscapes.

Bhoramdeo Temple

An ancient temple complex famous for its detailed stone carvings and historic architecture.

Sirpur

An important archaeological site known for ancient Buddhist, Hindu and Jain monuments.

Kanger Valley National Park

A protected forest landscape known for limestone caves, biodiversity and natural scenery.

Bastar

A region celebrated for its indigenous cultures, traditional crafts, forests and festivals.

Taste of tradition

Traditional Food

Chhattisgarhi cuisine uses rice, lentils, seasonal vegetables and locally available ingredients.

Chila

A savoury pancake commonly prepared using rice or lentil batter.

Fara

A traditional steamed rice-flour preparation, often served as a snack.

Angakar Roti

A traditional thick flatbread prepared using rice flour.

Bafauri

A steamed snack made from seasoned ground chana dal.

Dehrori

A traditional sweet made with fermented rice and curd-based batter.

Dubki Kadhi

A regional preparation featuring lentil dumplings cooked in a seasoned gravy.

Art and celebrations

Festivals & Culture

Folk traditions, metal crafts, woodwork, dance and community celebrations are central to the state's cultural life.

Bastar Dussehra

A distinctive and lengthy regional festival centred on Goddess Danteshwari and celebrated with traditional ceremonies.

Madai Festival

A travelling regional festival celebrated by different communities with fairs, rituals and cultural gatherings.

Panthi Dance

A devotional folk dance associated with the Satnami community, featuring rhythmic movements and songs.

Raut Nacha

A traditional folk dance associated with the Yadav community and the festival season around Diwali.

Dhokra Art

A traditional metal craft made using the lost-wax casting technique.

Kosa Silk

A distinctive variety of tussar silk associated with Chhattisgarh's weaving heritage.
