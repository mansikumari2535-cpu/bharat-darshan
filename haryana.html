WELCOME TO HARYANA

The Land of Heritage and Courage

Haryana is a state in northern India, formed on 1 November 1966. It is known for its agricultural contribution, sporting traditions, historic landmarks and deep cultural roots.

The ancient land of Kurukshetra is closely associated with the Mahabharata and the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita. Haryana also has a strong tradition of folk music, wrestling, rural fairs and community celebrations.

Famous Places to Visit

Kurukshetra

A major historical and religious destination associated with the Mahabharata. Brahma Sarovar and Jyotisar are among its well-known attractions.

Sultanpur National Park

Located near Gurugram, this national park is famous for migratory and resident birds. It is an important destination for nature lovers and birdwatchers.

Pinjore Gardens

Also known as Yadavindra Gardens, this historic garden near Panchkula features terraces, fountains and beautiful landscaping.

Morni Hills

A scenic hill destination in Panchkula district, known for green surroundings, peaceful views and its lakes.

Rakhigarhi

One of the important archaeological sites of the Indus Valley Civilisation, offering insights into an ancient urban settlement.

Panipat

A historic city remembered for three major battles that influenced the course of Indian history. Its museum and historic monuments attract visitors.

Badkhal Lake

Located near Faridabad, this lake area is known for its natural setting. Visitors should check local conditions before planning a visit.

Sheikh Chilli's Tomb

Located in Thanesar near Kurukshetra, this elegant Mughal-era complex is known for its architecture and peaceful gardens.

Traditional Haryanvi Food

Bajra Khichdi

A traditional rural dish prepared with pearl millet and commonly served with curd or homemade accompaniments.

Hara Dhania Cholia

A seasonal preparation made with fresh green chickpeas, herbs and spices.

Bathua Raita

A refreshing yoghurt-based side dish prepared with bathua, a leafy green commonly used in North Indian cooking.

Besan Masala Roti

A flavourful flatbread made using gram flour and spices, often enjoyed with curd or vegetables.

Churma

A traditional sweet preparation made from crushed wheat bread, ghee and jaggery or sugar.

Kadhi Pakora

A popular yoghurt and gram-flour curry served with soft or crispy pakoras and rice.

Festivals and Celebrations

Teej

A traditional monsoon festival celebrated with folk songs, gatherings, colourful decorations and cultural activities.

Surajkund International Crafts Mela

An internationally recognised fair near Faridabad that showcases handicrafts, handlooms, regional cuisines and cultural performances.

Gita Jayanti

Celebrated especially in Kurukshetra, this occasion honours the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita through religious and cultural programmes.

Baisakhi

A harvest celebration observed with community gatherings, traditional music and regional festivities.

Art, Sports and Cultural Heritage

Haryana is famous for its folk traditions, including Ragini singing, Saang folk theatre and energetic dances such as Dhamal and Phag. Traditional embroidery, pottery and handicrafts also form part of its cultural identity.

Wrestling, kabaddi, athletics and other sports have a significant place in Haryana's sporting culture. The state has produced many nationally and internationally recognised athletes.

Best Time to Explore Haryana

October to March is generally a comfortable period for sightseeing in Haryana. Summers can be very hot, while monsoon conditions vary across the state.
