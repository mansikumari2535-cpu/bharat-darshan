Explore the heart of the Deccan, where historic monuments, grand forts, rich traditions and modern city life come together.

Welcome to Telangana

Telangana is a state in southern India, known for its historic Deccan heritage, distinctive cuisine, traditional arts and growing technology sector. Hyderabad, its capital, is famous for the Charminar, historic markets and modern industries.

The state has a varied landscape of plateaus, rivers, forests and historic settlements. Telugu and Urdu are its official languages.

History of Telangana

Telangana has a long history shaped by several important Deccan dynasties and kingdoms. The Satavahanas, Kakatiyas, Bahmani rulers, Qutb Shahis and Asaf Jahi Nizams all influenced the region's political and cultural development.

The Kakatiya dynasty left a lasting architectural legacy, particularly at Warangal. Hyderabad developed as an important centre of the Deccan under the Qutb Shahi dynasty and later the Nizams.

Telangana became India's twenty-ninth state on 2 June 2014, following the reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh.

Famous Tourist Destinations

The capital city, famous for Charminar, Golconda Fort and historic markets.

Known for its historic fort, Kakatiya architecture and Thousand Pillar Temple.

A UNESCO World Heritage Site celebrated for its remarkable Kakatiya-era architecture.

Bhadrachalam

A well-known pilgrimage town associated with the Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy Temple.

Nagarjuna Sagar

Known for its major dam, reservoir and nearby archaeological heritage.

Bogatha Waterfall

A scenic waterfall surrounded by green landscapes in the state's forest region.

Culture and Traditions

Telangana has a vibrant cultural identity shaped by Telugu, Urdu and Deccan traditions. Bathukamma, a floral festival celebrated by women, is one of the state's most distinctive cultural celebrations.

Bonalu is another important festival, especially in Hyderabad and surrounding areas. Perini Shivatandavam, a traditional dance form associated with the Kakatiya period, is also part of Telangana's cultural heritage.

Famous Food of Telangana

Hyderabadi Biryani

A celebrated rice dish associated with Hyderabad's culinary heritage.

Haleem

A slow-cooked dish widely enjoyed in Hyderabad, especially during Ramadan.

Sarva Pindi

A traditional savoury pancake made with rice flour, peanuts and spices.

Sakinalu

A traditional crunchy snack prepared during festivals and celebrations.

Festivals of Telangana

Bathukamma and Bonalu are among Telangana's best-known festivals. Other celebrations include Sammakka Saralamma Jatara, one of the region's major tribal gatherings, along with Diwali, Eid and Sankranti.

Wildlife and Natural Heritage

Telangana has forests, river valleys, rocky landscapes and important wildlife habitats. Amrabad Tiger Reserve and Kawal Tiger Reserve are among its major protected areas.

The Godavari and Krishna river systems support agriculture, wetlands and diverse ecosystems across the state.
