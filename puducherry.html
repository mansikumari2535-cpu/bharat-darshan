WELCOME TO PUDUCHERRY

About Puducherry

Puducherry is a Union Territory on the southeastern coast of India. It is known for its French colonial heritage, seaside promenades, colourful streets, temples, churches and distinctive cultural identity.

The Union Territory consists of four geographically separate regions: Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam. Puducherry town is its capital.

HISTORY

A Unique Colonial Heritage

Puducherry has a long history of maritime trade and cultural exchange. The French established an important settlement here in the seventeenth century, and the region remained under French administration for several centuries.

The French territories were transferred to Indian administration de facto on 1 November 1954. The legal transfer was completed in 1962. The four former French settlements were later organised as the Union Territory of Puducherry.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

Famous Tourist Destinations

Promenade Beach

A popular seaside stretch known for its waterfront views and evening walks.

French Quarter

A historic neighbourhood featuring colonial-era buildings, colourful streets and distinctive architecture.

Auroville

An international township near Puducherry, known for its experimental community and the Matrimandir.

Paradise Beach

A scenic beach known for its sandy coastline and peaceful surroundings.

Arikamedu

An archaeological site associated with ancient maritime trade between India and the Mediterranean world.

Basilica of the Sacred Heart of Jesus

A prominent church known for its Gothic architectural features and stained-glass windows.

Manakula Vinayagar Temple

A historic Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Ganesha.

Chunnambar Boat House

A popular starting point for boat rides and visits to nearby coastal attractions.

CULTURE & TRADITIONS

A Blend of Indian and French Influences

Puducherry has a diverse cultural identity shaped by Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and French influences. Its streets, architecture, festivals and everyday traditions reflect this combination.

Pongal, Diwali, Christmas and other festivals are celebrated by communities across the territory.

FOOD & FLAVOURS

Traditional Cuisine

Puducherry's cuisine blends South Indian and French influences. Popular food traditions include dosa, idli, seafood preparations, Tamil-style meals, baked goods and French-inspired dishes.

BEACHES & NATURE

Coastal Beauty

The territory has beaches, backwaters, coastal wetlands and marine environments. These natural spaces support local biodiversity and offer opportunities for responsible tourism.

Protecting coastal ecosystems and reducing plastic pollution are important for preserving Puducherry's natural heritage.
