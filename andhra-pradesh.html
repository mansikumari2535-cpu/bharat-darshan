Andhra Pradesh

The Land of Culture, Temples, Classical Dance and Coastal Beauty.

Discover Andhra Pradesh

A Land of Rich Heritage

Andhra Pradesh is a state in southeastern India, known for its ancient temples, Telugu literature, classical dance, beautiful coastline and rich traditions. Its capital region is Amaravati.

Explore the state

Famous Places

Discover the historic, spiritual and natural attractions of Andhra Pradesh.

Tirupati

Home to the famous Sri Venkateswara Temple, one of India's most visited pilgrimage destinations.

Amaravati

An important historic and cultural centre associated with Buddhist heritage and the Krishna River.

Visakhapatnam

A coastal city known for its beaches, harbour, hills and scenic landscapes.

Araku Valley

A beautiful hill region famous for coffee plantations, valleys and natural scenery.

Lepakshi

Known for its historic temple architecture, murals and remarkable stone sculptures.

Gandikota

A dramatic landscape featuring a historic fort overlooking a deep gorge.

Taste of tradition

Traditional Food

Andhra cuisine is famous for its distinctive spices, rice dishes, pickles and regional flavours.

Gongura Pachadi

A popular tangy chutney prepared using gongura leaves.

Pulihora

A traditional tamarind-flavoured rice dish prepared with spices and peanuts.

Andhra Pesarattu

A savoury green gram dosa commonly enjoyed as a breakfast dish.

Pootharekulu

A delicate traditional sweet associated with Atreyapuram.

Art and celebrations

Festivals & Culture

Andhra Pradesh celebrates a vibrant mix of religious, agricultural and regional festivals.

Ugadi

The Telugu New Year, celebrated with traditional foods and festive gatherings.

Sankranti

A harvest festival celebrated with colourful decorations, traditional foods and cultural activities.

Kuchipudi

A celebrated Indian classical dance form that originated in Andhra Pradesh.

Kalamkari

A traditional textile art known for intricate hand-painted or block-printed designs.
