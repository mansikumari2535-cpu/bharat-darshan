Odisha

Discover ancient temples, coastal landscapes, classical dance, colourful festivals and the timeless heritage of eastern India.

Welcome to Odisha

Odisha is a state on India's eastern coast, bordered by the Bay of Bengal. Its capital is Bhubaneswar, often called the Temple City because of its remarkable collection of historic temples. Odia is the official language.

The state is known for its ancient history, religious traditions, classical arts, handloom textiles, forests, rivers and long coastline. Cuttack, Puri, Sambalpur and Rourkela are among its important cities.

History and Heritage

Odisha has a long history associated with ancient Kalinga, which became famous in the time of Emperor Ashoka. The Kalinga War had a major influence on Ashoka's life and his later support for Buddhism.

The region was later shaped by several dynasties, including the Eastern Gangas and the Gajapatis. The Sun Temple at Konark, the temples of Bhubaneswar and the Jagannath Temple at Puri are important parts of Odisha's architectural and cultural heritage. Odisha became a separate province in 1936 and a state in independent India.

Culture and Traditions

Odisha is famous for Odissi, one of India's classical dance forms. Its graceful movements and expressive storytelling are closely connected with the state's artistic and devotional traditions.

Rath Yatra in Puri is one of the state's best-known festivals. Durga Puja, Raja Parba, Nuakhai and Bali Jatra are also important celebrations that reflect the region's diverse traditions.

Pattachitra painting, silver filigree work, stone carving and handloom weaving are among Odisha's recognised crafts.

Traditional Cuisine

Odia cuisine is known for its rice-based meals, lentils, vegetables and regional flavours. Dalma, prepared with lentils and vegetables, is a well-known traditional dish.

Other popular foods include pakhala bhata, chhena poda, santula and a variety of traditional sweets. Food offerings associated with the Jagannath Temple are also an important part of Odisha's culinary heritage.

Famous Tourist Places

Puri

A major coastal pilgrimage city known for the Jagannath Temple and Rath Yatra.

Konark

Home to the famous Sun Temple, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Bhubaneswar

The capital city, known for ancient temples and archaeological heritage.

Chilika Lake

A vast brackish-water lagoon famous for migratory birds and its biodiversity.

Similipal

A protected forest landscape known for wildlife and waterfalls.

Dhauli

A historic site associated with the Kalinga War and Ashoka's transformation.

Wildlife and Nature

Odisha has a diverse natural environment of forests, wetlands, rivers and coastal habitats. Similipal National Park and Bhitarkanika National Park are important protected areas.

Chilika Lake supports large numbers of resident and migratory birds. The Gahirmatha coast is especially important as a nesting site for Olive Ridley sea turtles.
