WELCOME TO GUJARAT

The Land of Heritage and Vibrant Traditions

Gujarat is a state in western India known for its rich history, distinctive architecture, lively festivals and entrepreneurial spirit. From the ancient ports of its coastline to the vast white salt desert of Kutch, the state offers an extraordinary variety of landscapes and experiences.

Gujarat is also associated with Mahatma Gandhi, whose birthplace was Porbandar. Its cultural identity is reflected in traditional textiles, folk music, handicrafts, temples and community celebrations.

Famous Places to Visit

Rann of Kutch

A vast seasonal salt marsh famous for its brilliant white landscape. The region becomes especially lively during the Rann Utsav, with cultural performances, crafts and local experiences.

Statue of Unity

Located near Ekta Nagar, this monumental statue honours Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. The surrounding area includes gardens, viewpoints and visitor attractions.

Gir National Park

One of India's most important wildlife destinations, Gir is internationally known as the natural home of the Asiatic lion.

Somnath Temple

A celebrated pilgrimage destination on the Arabian Sea coast, Somnath is known for its historic temple and beautiful coastal setting.

Dwarka

An important Hindu pilgrimage city associated with Lord Krishna. Dwarkadhish Temple is among its best-known landmarks.

Rani ki Vav

This remarkable stepwell in Patan is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its detailed stone carvings showcase the brilliance of historic Indian architecture.

Ahmedabad

Gujarat's largest city is known for its historic architecture, traditional neighbourhoods and the UNESCO-listed historic city.

Champaner-Pavagadh

A UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring historic mosques, forts, palaces and archaeological remains set in a scenic landscape.

Traditional Gujarati Food

Dhokla

A soft, savoury steamed snack made from fermented batter, usually served with chutney and a light seasoning.

Thepla

A popular Gujarati flatbread prepared with spices and commonly enjoyed with curd or pickle.

Undhiyu

A traditional mixed vegetable dish prepared with seasonal ingredients and aromatic spices.

Gujarati Kadhi

A comforting yoghurt-based curry with a distinctive sweet, tangy and mildly spiced flavour.

Handvo

A savoury baked or pan-cooked cake made with lentils, rice and vegetables.

Fafda and Jalebi

A much-loved combination of crispy gram-flour snack and sweet jalebi, especially popular during festivals.

Festivals and Celebrations

Navratri

One of Gujarat's most colourful celebrations, featuring traditional Garba and Dandiya Raas dances.

Uttarayan

The kite festival celebrated in January transforms rooftops and open spaces into colourful scenes across the state.

Rann Utsav

A seasonal cultural festival showcasing the crafts, music, dance and traditions of the Kutch region.

Art, Crafts and Cultural Heritage

Gujarat has a remarkable tradition of handmade arts. Patola weaving from Patan, Bandhani tie-dye textiles, Kutchi embroidery and Rogan art are among its distinctive craft traditions.

Folk music, Garba, Dandiya Raas and regional storytelling continue to bring communities together. The state's diverse landscapes and historic settlements make it an important part of India's cultural heritage.

Best Time to Explore Gujarat

The cooler months from October to March are generally comfortable for exploring many parts of Gujarat. Weather conditions vary across the state's desert, coastline and inland regions, so check local conditions before planning a trip.
