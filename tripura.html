Welcome to Tripura

Tripura is a state in northeastern India, surrounded on three sides by Bangladesh and connected to the rest of India through the northeastern region. It is known for its royal history, diverse communities, bamboo crafts and scenic landscapes.

Agartala is the capital of Tripura. Bengali, Kokborok and English are among the languages used in the state, reflecting its cultural and linguistic diversity.

History of Tripura

Tripura has a long history associated with the Manikya dynasty, which ruled the region for centuries. The royal family played an important role in shaping its administration, architecture, literature and cultural traditions.

The former princely state joined the Indian Union in 1949. Tripura became a Union Territory in 1956 and attained full statehood on 21 January 1972.

The state's historic monuments, temples and palaces preserve important chapters of its royal and cultural past.

Culture and Traditions

Tripura has a diverse cultural heritage shaped by Bengali traditions and numerous indigenous communities. Traditional music, dance, weaving and bamboo craftsmanship are important parts of everyday cultural life.

Hojagiri is a famous dance form associated with the Reang (Bru) community. Other traditional dances and celebrations reflect the state's many communities and customs.

Famous Food of Tripura

Mui Borok

The traditional cuisine of Tripura, featuring distinctive local ingredients and flavours.

Chakhwi

A traditional dish prepared with bamboo shoots and other local ingredients.

Wahan Mosdeng

A traditional pork preparation associated with Tripuri cuisine.

Gudok

A traditional dish made with fermented fish and vegetables.

Festivals of Tripura

Kharchi Puja is one of Tripura's best-known festivals. Garia Puja is another important celebration, especially among indigenous communities. Durga Puja and other regional festivals are also celebrated with enthusiasm.

Wildlife and Natural Heritage

Tripura has green hills, forests, wetlands and rich biodiversity. Sepahijala Wildlife Sanctuary, Trishna Wildlife Sanctuary and Clouded Leopard National Park are important protected areas.

The state's forests support many bird and animal species. Bamboo and cane are also important natural resources and contribute to local crafts and livelihoods.
