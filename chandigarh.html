WELCOME TO CHANDIGARH

About Chandigarh

Chandigarh is a Union Territory in northern India and serves as the shared capital of Punjab and Haryana. It is widely recognised for its planned layout, organised sectors, green spaces and modern architecture.

The city was planned after India's independence and became an important example of modern urban planning in the country.

HISTORY

The Story of Chandigarh

After the Partition of India in 1947, East Punjab needed a new capital. Chandigarh was developed as a planned city to fulfil this need. The city's master plan was strongly associated with architect Le Corbusier and his team.

On 1 November 1966, following the reorganisation of Punjab, Chandigarh became a Union Territory and the shared capital of Punjab and Haryana.

CULTURE & LIFESTYLE

Culture and Traditions

Chandigarh has a diverse population influenced by Punjabi and neighbouring North Indian cultures. Festivals such as Baisakhi, Diwali, Holi and Lohri are celebrated with enthusiasm.

The city also has a lively cultural scene, with museums, theatres, educational institutions and public events.

FOOD & FLAVOURS

Food of Chandigarh

The city's food culture reflects the flavours of Punjab and North India. Popular choices include chole bhature, parathas, sarson da saag, makki di roti and a variety of traditional sweets.

ARCHITECTURE & GREEN SPACES

A City Designed for Living

Chandigarh is divided into organised sectors and is known for its planned roads, open spaces and distinctive modernist architecture. Its design has made it an important reference point in the history of urban planning in India.
