WELCOME TO LAKSHADWEEP

About Lakshadweep

Lakshadweep is a group of islands located in the Arabian Sea, off the southwestern coast of India. It is India's smallest Union Territory by area and is known for its coral atolls, lagoons and clear coastal waters.

Kavaratti is the capital of Lakshadweep. The territory consists of 36 islands, including inhabited islands, uninhabited islands and coral islets.

HISTORY

History of the Islands

Lakshadweep has a history shaped by maritime trade, fishing communities and cultural connections across the Arabian Sea. Islam became an important part of the islands' history and cultural identity.

The islands were administered as part of the former Laccadive, Minicoy and Amindivi Islands. In 1973, the territory was officially named Lakshadweep.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

Famous Island Destinations

Kavaratti

The capital island, known for its lagoons, mosques and beautiful coastal scenery.

Agatti Island

Known for its scenic coastline, coral reefs and airport connecting the islands with mainland India.

Bangaram Island

A picturesque island surrounded by clear lagoons and coral formations.

Minicoy Island

Known for its distinctive cultural traditions, lighthouse and traditional boat-building heritage.

Kalpeni Island

Famous for its lagoon, coral surroundings and coastal landscapes.

Kadmat Island

A long, narrow island known for its beaches and surrounding marine environment.

CULTURE & TRADITIONS

Island Culture

Lakshadweep has a distinctive island culture influenced by maritime connections and traditions from the Malabar Coast. Music, folk dances, traditional boats and community celebrations are important parts of local life.

The cultural traditions of Minicoy have their own distinctive characteristics and are closely connected with the island's history and language.

FOOD & FLAVOURS

Traditional Island Cuisine

Coconut, rice and seafood are important ingredients in Lakshadweep cuisine. Local dishes often reflect the flavours of Kerala and the islands' maritime food traditions.

MARINE LIFE & ENVIRONMENT

A World of Coral Reefs

Lakshadweep's coral reefs support a wide variety of marine life, including colourful reef fish, sea turtles and other ocean species. Its lagoons and coastal ecosystems are environmentally important.

Protecting coral reefs, reducing marine pollution and conserving coastal habitats are essential for the future of these islands.
