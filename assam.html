Discover Assam

A Land Shaped by the Brahmaputra

Assam is a northeastern Indian state known for the mighty Brahmaputra River, lush tea plantations, rich biodiversity, silk weaving and colourful festivals. Its cultural heritage reflects the traditions of many communities living across the Brahmaputra and Barak valleys.

Explore the state

Famous Places

From ancient temples to river islands and wildlife reserves, Assam offers many memorable destinations.

Kaziranga National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site celebrated for its population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros and rich wildlife.

Majuli

A large river island in the Brahmaputra, known for its Vaishnavite monasteries called satras and traditional arts.

Kamakhya Temple

An important Shakti pilgrimage site situated on Nilachal Hill in Guwahati.

Sivasagar

A historic town associated with the Ahom kingdom, featuring monuments, tanks and temples.

Manas National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its forests, grasslands and diverse wildlife.

Jorhat

A cultural centre surrounded by tea gardens and known for its connection with Assam's tea industry.

Taste of tradition

Traditional Food

Assamese cuisine is known for its balanced flavours, rice-based meals, fresh herbs, fish and regional ingredients.

Khar

A distinctive Assamese preparation traditionally made with an alkaline ingredient and served as part of a meal.

Masor Tenga

A light, tangy fish curry often flavoured with tomatoes, lemon or other sour ingredients.

Poita Bhat

A traditional rice preparation associated with warm-weather meals in Assam.

Pitha

Traditional rice cakes prepared in different forms, especially during Bihu celebrations.

Art and celebrations

Festivals & Culture

Assam's cultural identity is shaped by music, dance, handloom traditions and festivals celebrated throughout the year.

Rongali Bihu

The spring festival marking the Assamese New Year and the agricultural season, celebrated with music and dance.

Bhogali Bihu

A harvest festival associated with community feasts, traditional games and seasonal celebrations.

Kati Bihu

A quieter agricultural observance associated with prayers for a good harvest.

Assam Silk

Assam is renowned for Muga, Eri and Pat silk, which are important parts of its handloom heritage.

Sattriya Dance

An Indian classical dance form that developed in Assam's Vaishnavite monasteries.

Gamosa

A traditional woven cloth that holds a special place in Assamese identity and hospitality.
