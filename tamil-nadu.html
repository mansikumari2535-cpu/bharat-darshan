Discover a land of ancient temples, timeless Tamil traditions, classical music, colourful festivals and beautiful coastlines.

Welcome to Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is a state in southern India, known for its ancient Dravidian temples, classical arts, rich literature and distinctive cuisine. Its long coastline along the Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean adds to its natural and cultural diversity.

Chennai is the capital and one of India's major metropolitan cities. Tamil, one of the world's oldest classical languages, is the official language of the state.

History of Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu has a remarkable history shaped by ancient Tamil civilisation and powerful dynasties. The Cholas, Cheras and Pandyas played major roles in the region's political, cultural and artistic development.

The Pallavas were also influential, leaving behind remarkable temple architecture at places such as Mahabalipuram. Later, the Vijayanagara Empire and the Nayak rulers contributed to the region's forts, temples and cultural traditions.

The modern state was originally known as Madras State and was officially renamed Tamil Nadu in 1969.

Famous Tourist Destinations

Chennai

The capital city, known for Marina Beach, historic buildings, museums and cultural institutions.

Madurai

Famous for the magnificent Meenakshi Amman Temple and its historic streets.

Mahabalipuram

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for ancient rock-cut monuments and shore temples.

Thanjavur

Known for the Brihadisvara Temple, Chola heritage and traditional art.

Ooty

A popular hill station known for tea gardens, mountain views and its historic railway.

Kanyakumari

Famous for its coastal scenery and the meeting of the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean.

Culture and Traditions

Tamil Nadu has a rich cultural identity rooted in Tamil language, literature, music, dance and temple traditions. Bharatanatyam, one of India's major classical dance forms, has strong historical connections with the region.

Carnatic music, traditional crafts, bronze sculpture and colourful temple festivals are important parts of its heritage. Pongal is one of the state's most celebrated harvest festivals.

Famous Food of Tamil Nadu

Idli

Soft steamed rice cakes commonly served with sambar and chutney.

Dosa

A thin, crispy fermented rice and lentil crepe enjoyed across the state.

Pongal

A traditional rice and lentil dish, prepared in both savoury and sweet styles.

Chettinad Cuisine

A distinctive regional cuisine known for its aromatic spices and varied dishes.

Festivals of Tamil Nadu

Pongal is a major harvest festival celebrated with traditional food, decorated homes and community gatherings. Other important celebrations include Tamil New Year, Karthigai Deepam and temple festivals such as the Madurai Chithirai Festival.

Wildlife and Natural Heritage

Tamil Nadu has diverse natural habitats, including forests, grasslands, wetlands and coastal ecosystems. Mudumalai Tiger Reserve, Anamalai Tiger Reserve and Gulf of Mannar Marine National Park are among its important protected areas.

The Nilgiri Hills are part of the Western Ghats, a globally recognised biodiversity hotspot. The region supports many unique plants, birds and wildlife species.
