Welcome to West Bengal

West Bengal is a state in eastern India, known for its distinctive Bengali culture, rich literary heritage, historic cities and diverse natural landscapes. It stretches from the Himalayan foothills in the north to the coastal plains and Sundarbans in the south.

Kolkata is the capital of West Bengal and an important centre of literature, education, art and commerce. Bengali is the principal official language of the state.

History of West Bengal

West Bengal has a long history shaped by ancient kingdoms, trade networks and major cultural movements. The region was part of important historical empires, including the Maurya and Gupta empires.

During the medieval period, Bengal developed as an important centre of regional kingdoms and later came under Mughal rule. The British East India Company established its influence in Bengal, and Kolkata became a major colonial administrative and commercial centre.

Bengal played a significant role in India's freedom movement. Following the partition of British India in 1947, West Bengal became a state within independent India.

Culture and Traditions

West Bengal has a celebrated cultural heritage in literature, music, theatre, cinema, painting and intellectual life. The works of Rabindranath Tagore and other Bengali writers have had a lasting influence on Indian culture.

Baul music, Chhau dance, terracotta art, traditional weaving and hand-crafted items are important parts of its artistic traditions. Durga Puja is one of the state's most famous cultural celebrations.

Famous Food of West Bengal

A traditional Bengali fish curry prepared with spices and vegetables.

Shorshe Ilish

A celebrated preparation of hilsa fish cooked with mustard.

Rosogolla

A famous Bengali sweet made from soft chhena balls in sugar syrup.

Mishti Doi

A traditional sweetened fermented yoghurt dessert.

Festivals of West Bengal

Durga Puja is the most widely recognised festival of West Bengal, celebrated with elaborate decorations, artistic pandals, cultural performances and community gatherings. Other important celebrations include Kali Puja, Poila Boishakh, Saraswati Puja and Rath Yatra.

Wildlife and Natural Heritage

West Bengal has diverse natural environments, including Himalayan forests, tea-growing hills, river wetlands, coastal areas and mangrove ecosystems.

The Sundarbans National Park is famous for its mangrove ecosystem and Bengal tiger habitat. Jaldapara National Park and Gorumara National Park are also important wildlife destinations, known for Indian rhinoceroses and other species.
