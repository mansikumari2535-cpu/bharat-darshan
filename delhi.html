WELCOME TO DELHI

About Delhi

Delhi is the National Capital Territory of India and an important political, cultural and historical centre. It is home to national institutions, historic monuments, educational centres and diverse communities.

New Delhi, located within the National Capital Territory, is the capital of India. Delhi's landscape includes historic neighbourhoods, planned areas, markets, parks and modern urban developments.

HISTORY

Centuries of History

Delhi has been an important centre of power and culture for many centuries. Different dynasties and empires shaped the region, leaving behind forts, mosques, tombs, gardens and other monuments.

During British rule, New Delhi was developed as the imperial capital and inaugurated in 1931. After independence in 1947, New Delhi became the capital of independent India. Delhi received its present National Capital Territory status in 1991 under the Constitution (Sixty-ninth Amendment) Act, 1991.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

Famous Tourist Destinations

Red Fort

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and historic Mughal fort associated with India's independence celebrations.

India Gate

A prominent war memorial and one of the best-known landmarks of New Delhi.

Qutub Minar

A UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its impressive medieval minaret and surrounding monuments.

Humayun's Tomb

A beautiful Mughal-era garden tomb and UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Lotus Temple

A distinctive place of worship known for its flower-shaped architecture and welcoming atmosphere.

Jama Masjid

One of India's most significant historic mosques, built during the Mughal period.

Purana Qila

An important historic fort with impressive gateways and archaeological significance.

National Museum

A major museum displaying collections connected with India's art, history and archaeology.

CULTURE & FESTIVALS

A City of Many Traditions

Delhi's culture reflects influences from across India. People celebrate festivals such as Diwali, Holi, Eid, Gurpurab, Christmas and many regional festivals.

The city is also known for its theatres, art galleries, music, literary events and traditional crafts.

FOOD & FLAVOURS

The Famous Food of Delhi

Delhi has a rich food culture with influences from Mughlai, Punjabi and other regional cuisines. Popular dishes include chole bhature, parathas, kebabs, chaat, jalebi and many traditional sweets.

Markets and food streets across Old Delhi are especially known for their long-standing culinary traditions.

MARKETS & CITY LIFE

Shopping and Urban Experiences

Chandni Chowk, Connaught Place, Dilli Haat and other shopping destinations offer everything from traditional crafts and textiles to modern shopping experiences.

Delhi also has extensive metro connectivity, public parks and cultural spaces that connect its historic and modern areas.
