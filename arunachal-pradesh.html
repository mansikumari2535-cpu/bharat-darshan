Arunachal Pradesh

The Land of the Dawn-Lit Mountains, rich tribal traditions and Himalayan beauty.

Discover Arunachal Pradesh

A World of Mountains and Traditions

Arunachal Pradesh is India's northeasternmost state, known for its snow-covered mountains, green valleys, rivers, monasteries and diverse indigenous communities. It shares international borders with Bhutan, China and Myanmar.

Explore the state

Famous Places

Discover spectacular landscapes, cultural landmarks and peaceful mountain destinations.

Tawang

Famous for Tawang Monastery, mountain scenery and its rich Buddhist heritage.

Itanagar

The state capital, known for Ita Fort, cultural institutions and nearby natural attractions.

Ziro Valley

A scenic valley known for Apatani culture, green landscapes and traditional villages.

Bomdila

A mountain town offering Himalayan views, monasteries and local handicrafts.

Namdapha National Park

A large protected area recognised for its remarkable biodiversity and forests.

Mechuka

A beautiful remote valley with mountain scenery, rivers and traditional settlements.

Taste of tradition

Traditional Food

Food traditions vary among the many communities of Arunachal Pradesh, often featuring local vegetables, rice, herbs and regional ingredients.

Thukpa

A comforting noodle soup enjoyed in Himalayan regions.

Momos

Steamed dumplings prepared with different fillings and served with chutney.

Apong

A traditional rice-based beverage associated with several communities. It is included here as cultural heritage, not as a recommendation for consumption.

Pika Pila

A traditional pickle associated with the Apatani community, often prepared using bamboo shoot and other local ingredients.

Art and celebrations

Festivals & Culture

Arunachal Pradesh is home to many indigenous communities, each with distinct languages, customs, crafts and celebrations.

Losar

A New Year festival celebrated by Buddhist communities, including the Monpa people.

Nyokum

A major festival of the Nyishi community, associated with prayers for harmony and prosperity.

Solung

An important agricultural festival celebrated by the Adi community.

Tribal Handicrafts

Traditional weaving, bamboo work, wood carving and other crafts reflect the region's cultural diversity.
