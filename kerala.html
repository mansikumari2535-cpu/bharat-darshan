Kerala

Discover the green landscapes, peaceful backwaters, rich traditions and vibrant culture of God's Own Country.

Welcome to Kerala

Kerala is a beautiful state located on the southwestern coast of India, along the Arabian Sea. Known for its lush greenery, palm-lined beaches, hills, rivers and backwaters, Kerala has a distinctive natural and cultural identity. Its capital is Thiruvananthapuram.

The state is known for its high literacy, traditional art forms, diverse communities and strong connection with nature. Malayalam is its official language, and the region has a long history of maritime trade and cultural exchange.

History and Heritage

Kerala's history includes ancient port settlements, regional kingdoms and centuries of overseas trade. Spices such as pepper and cardamom attracted merchants from different parts of the world. The Chera dynasty played an important role in the region's early history.

Later, the area saw the influence of several local rulers and European trading powers. Kerala became a state on 1 November 1956, following the reorganisation of Indian states.

Culture and Traditions

Kerala's culture is expressed through classical dance, theatre, music, festivals and traditional crafts. Kathakali is famous for its elaborate costumes, expressive movements and storytelling. Mohiniyattam is another classical dance form associated with the state.

Onam is one of Kerala's major festivals. It is celebrated with flower decorations, traditional games, cultural performances and the famous Onam Sadya feast.

Traditional Cuisine

Kerala cuisine is known for its use of coconut, curry leaves, rice and regional spices. Popular dishes include appam with vegetable stew, puttu with kadala curry, idiyappam and Kerala-style vegetable dishes.

Onam Sadya is a traditional vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf, featuring a variety of dishes and accompaniments.

Famous Tourist Places

Alappuzha

Known for its backwaters, canals and houseboat experiences.

Munnar

A hill destination famous for tea gardens and mountain scenery.

Kochi

A historic port city known for its heritage streets and waterfront.

Wayanad

Known for forests, waterfalls, wildlife and scenic hills.

Wildlife and Nature

Kerala is home to rich biodiversity, with tropical forests, wetlands and the Western Ghats. Periyar Tiger Reserve, Silent Valley National Park and Eravikulam National Park are important protected areas. The state's natural landscapes support elephants, birds, deer and many other species.

Discover More of Kerala

From misty hills and wildlife reserves to historic towns and peaceful backwaters, Kerala offers a remarkable view of India's natural and cultural diversity.
