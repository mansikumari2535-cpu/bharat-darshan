WELCOME TO JAMMU AND KASHMIR

About Jammu and Kashmir

Jammu and Kashmir is a Union Territory in northern India, known for its Himalayan landscapes, valleys, rivers, lakes and cultural heritage. Its summer capital is Srinagar and its winter capital is Jammu.

The region includes the Jammu division and the Kashmir division. Each has its own distinctive geography, traditions, architecture and food culture.

HISTORY

A Region with a Long History

Jammu and Kashmir has a rich history shaped by ancient kingdoms, religious traditions, trade routes and several periods of political change. Its architecture and cultural heritage reflect many historical influences.

Under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019, the former state was reorganised into the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union Territory of Ladakh, effective from 31 October 2019.

PLACES TO EXPLORE

Famous Tourist Destinations

Dal Lake

One of Srinagar's best-known attractions, famous for its houseboats, shikaras and mountain views.

Gulmarg

A scenic mountain destination known for meadows, winter snow and panoramic Himalayan views.

Pahalgam

A beautiful valley town surrounded by mountains, rivers and green landscapes.

Mughal Gardens

Srinagar's historic gardens, including Shalimar Bagh and Nishat Bagh, are known for their terraces and fountains.

Vaishno Devi

A major pilgrimage destination in the Trikuta Hills near Katra.

Patnitop

A hill destination in the Jammu region known for forested surroundings and mountain scenery.

Shankaracharya Temple

A historic temple on a hill overlooking Srinagar and Dal Lake.

Jammu Bahu Fort

A historic fort overlooking the Tawi River and the city of Jammu.

CULTURE & TRADITIONS

A Rich Cultural Heritage

Jammu and Kashmir is home to diverse communities and traditions. Kashmiri, Dogra and other cultural influences can be seen in its music, festivals, clothing, crafts and architecture.

Traditional crafts include Pashmina weaving, carpet making, papier-mâché, wood carving and embroidery.

FOOD & FLAVOURS

Traditional Cuisine

The region is known for dishes such as Rogan Josh, Yakhni, Dum Aloo, Kalari cheese and the traditional Kashmiri Wazwan feast. Jammu also has its own distinctive Dogra food traditions.

NATURE & LANDSCAPES

Mountains, Valleys and Wildlife

The region contains forests, alpine meadows, rivers and mountain ecosystems. Dachigam National Park is known for the Hangul, also called the Kashmir stag.

These natural landscapes are important for biodiversity, conservation and local livelihoods.
