Goa

A Coastal Paradise of Sunlit Beaches, Historic Architecture and Colourful Traditions.

Discover Goa

Where Heritage Meets the Sea

Goa is India's smallest state by area, located along the Arabian Sea. It is known for its beautiful coastline, Konkani language, historic churches and temples, distinctive architecture, music and vibrant cultural traditions. Its history reflects the influence of several Indian kingdoms and centuries of Portuguese rule.

Explore the state

Famous Places

Discover Goa's beaches, historic landmarks, wildlife and architectural treasures.

Basilica of Bom Jesus

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in Old Goa, renowned for its Baroque architecture and religious significance.

Fort Aguada

A historic Portuguese-era coastal fort overlooking the Arabian Sea.

Panaji

The state capital, known for its colourful Latin Quarter, riverside setting and heritage buildings.

Dudhsagar Falls

A famous multi-tiered waterfall situated in the Western Ghats near the Goa-Karnataka border.

Palolem Beach

A scenic beach in South Goa known for its curved coastline and natural surroundings.

Fontainhas

Panaji's historic Latin Quarter, known for colourful Portuguese-influenced houses and narrow streets.

Taste of tradition

Traditional Food

Goan cuisine combines coastal ingredients, local spices, rice, coconut and a variety of regional cooking traditions.

Goan Fish Curry

A coastal curry commonly prepared with fish, coconut and regional spices.

Goan Prawn Balchão

A tangy, spicy preparation influenced by Portuguese-era culinary traditions.

Ros Omelette

A popular local street-food dish featuring an omelette served with a savoury gravy.

Bebinca

A traditional layered Goan dessert made with coconut milk, eggs, sugar and flour.

Sol Kadhi

A refreshing regional preparation made using kokum and coconut milk.

Patoleo

Traditional rice cakes steamed in turmeric leaves, often prepared for special occasions.

Art and celebrations

Festivals & Culture

Goa's cultural life blends Konkani traditions, folk performances, religious celebrations and historic influences.

Goa Carnival

A colourful annual celebration known for parades, music, costumes and community festivities.

Shigmo

A traditional spring festival featuring folk dances, music and colourful processions.

Feast of St. Francis Xavier

A major religious observance connected with the Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa.

Fugdi Dance

A traditional Goan folk dance performed by women during festivals and community celebrations.

Dhalo

A traditional folk dance performed by women, often during winter celebrations.

Goan Azulejos

Decorative painted ceramic tiles that form part of Goa's distinctive architectural heritage.
