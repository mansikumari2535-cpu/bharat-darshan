Welcome to Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is a state in northern India, known for its historical importance, religious heritage, architecture, agriculture and cultural diversity. The sacred River Ganga flows through several important cities of the state.

Lucknow is the capital of Uttar Pradesh. The state is home to globally recognised destinations such as the Taj Mahal in Agra, the ghats of Varanasi and the historic city of Ayodhya.

History of Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh has been an important centre of Indian history and civilisation for thousands of years. Ancient cities such as Varanasi, Mathura and Ayodhya are associated with major religious, philosophical and cultural traditions.

The region was part of important historical kingdoms and empires, including the Maurya and Gupta empires. During the medieval period, the Delhi Sultanate and Mughal Empire influenced its architecture, administration and culture.

The region played an important role in India's freedom movement. The modern state was established on 24 January 1950, when the former United Provinces were renamed Uttar Pradesh.

Famous Tourist Destinations

Agra

Home to the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and other remarkable Mughal monuments.

Varanasi

An ancient city known for its riverfront ghats, temples and spiritual traditions.

Ayodhya

A historic city associated with the Ramayana and important religious sites.

Lucknow

Known for Bara Imambara, historic architecture and its distinctive cultural heritage.

Mathura and Vrindavan

Important pilgrimage destinations associated with the life and traditions of Lord Krishna.

Sarnath

A major Buddhist pilgrimage site where Gautama Buddha delivered his first sermon.

Culture and Traditions

Uttar Pradesh has a diverse cultural heritage expressed through music, dance, literature, handicrafts and festivals. Kathak, one of India's major classical dance forms, has strong historical connections with the region.

Banarasi silk weaving, Lucknow's chikankari embroidery, traditional pottery and brass crafts are among its well-known artistic traditions.

Famous Food of Uttar Pradesh

Awadhi Biryani

A fragrant rice preparation associated with the culinary traditions of Lucknow.

Tunday Kebab

A famous Lucknow speciality known for its distinctive preparation and spices.

Banarasi Chaat

A collection of popular street-food preparations from Varanasi.

Petha

A well-known sweet speciality associated with Agra.

Festivals of Uttar Pradesh

Major festivals include Kumbh Mela, Diwali celebrations in Ayodhya, Holi in Mathura and Vrindavan, and the Dev Deepawali celebration in Varanasi. These events attract visitors from across India and around the world.

Wildlife and Natural Heritage

Uttar Pradesh has forests, wetlands, grasslands and river ecosystems. Dudhwa National Park is known for its rich wildlife, including tigers and swamp deer.

The National Chambal Sanctuary protects important river habitats and is known for the gharial, Gangetic river dolphin and several species of birds.
